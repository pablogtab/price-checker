# Repository history

This repository was published from an existing project snapshot in October 2026.
The initial import commits use synthetic author and committer timestamps spread
from October 2025 through May 2026, at the repository owner's request.
They organize existing source files; they do not represent the actual dates of
development or independently verified, runnable historical releases.

Local credentials, supplier sessions, catalog exports, business records, and
operational validation reports are excluded from version control.
